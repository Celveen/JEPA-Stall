\section{Conclusion}
Interaction-rich training data and execution-conditioned planning address complementary parts of closed-loop control. ECP uses observed stagnation to sustain correction with frozen or adaptive world models. Development results reach 77.7\% with AdaJEPA and 66.0\% with geometry-assisted contact optimization on frozen LeWM.

\paragraph{Future work.}
DAgger-inspired recovery-data aggregation and ACT-inspired persistent recovery intent remain prospective extensions. Independent training tasks, source-disjoint holdouts, collection factorials, and multiple training seeds would clarify their effects and uncertainty in paired gains~\cite{henderson2018matters,agarwal2021statistical}.
