\section{Conclusion}
We identified \emph{stalling} as a closed-loop failure of latent world-model planners whose goal objective retains no execution memory. \emph{Execution-Conditioned Planning} (ECP) turns episode history into a corrective signal: detecting stagnation, discouraging revisits through goal-preserving memory, and completing unfinished subspaces through temporary reweighting. ECP adds no learned parameters and supports fixed or online-adapted models. Paired with interaction-biased training, it improves manipulation, shift robustness, navigation, and deformable control across ResNet, LeWM, and DINO-WM, highlighting execution history as a useful resource for planning.
