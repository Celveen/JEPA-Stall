\section{Conclusion}
A stalled world-model controller reveals exactly where its plans fail, and Execution-Conditioned Planning turns that evidence into recovery. By remembering stalled states and temporarily reweighting the goal, ECP corrects the planning objective without adding learned parameters or changing the world model, and it works with frozen and online-adapting models alike. Together with interaction-biased data augmentation, it raises PushObj success to 72.3\% with frozen dynamics and 77.7\% with AdaJEPA, attains the best PushT robustness among all compared world-model controllers, and improves Maze navigation without regressions. Execution history is a rich and freely available signal for planning.

\paragraph{Future work.}
Future work includes shift-robust encoders for appearance changes such as PushT object color, aggregating verified recovery trajectories into training data, and learning temporally extended recovery behaviors (inspired by ACT). Source-disjoint holdouts and multiple training seeds will quantify uncertainty in the paired gains~\cite{henderson2018matters,agarwal2021statistical}.
