\section{Conclusion}
Latent world-model planners can stall by repeating failed plans. Execution-Conditioned Planning uses episode history to detect stagnation, penalize revisits, and emphasize unfinished goal components. It adds no learned parameters and works with fixed or online-adapted models. Together with interaction-biased training, it improves manipulation, navigation, and deformable-object planning across ResNet, LeWM, and DINO-WM controllers. These results highlight execution history as a useful planning signal. Future work includes shift-robust detection and learned recovery behaviors.
