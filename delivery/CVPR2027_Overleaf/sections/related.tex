\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}
\paragraph{World models and latent planning.}
Planning through a learned model dates back to Dyna and to model predictive control (MPC) with neural dynamics~\cite{sutton1991dyna,nagabandi2018neural,chua2018pets}. In visual control, it has moved from locally linear latent dynamics and predicted images~\cite{watter2015embed,finn2017foresight} to the learned latent spaces of World Models, PlaNet, Dreamer, and TD-MPC2~\cite{ha2018worldmodels,hafner2019planet,hafner2020dreamer,hansen2024tdmpc2}. Joint-embedding predictive architectures predict representations directly~\cite{assran2023ijepa,bardes2024vjepa,balestriero2025lejepa}, and DINO-WM, V-JEPA~2, and LeWorldModel show that such models support planning from image goals~\cite{zhou2025dinowm,assran2025vjepa2,maes2026lewm}. ECP is complementary: it takes the world model as given and decides, from execution, when and how the planning objective should change.

\paragraph{Shaping representations and data for planning.}
A second line of work improves the space in which planning takes place. Representations can be trained to preserve rewards and transitions or to support goal-directed planning~\cite{gelada2019deepmdp,farahmand2017valueaware,srinivas2018upn}. Building on perceptual straightening~\cite{henaff2019straightening}, Temporal Straightening aligns consecutive latent displacements~\cite{wang2026straightening}; AnisoWM shapes the latent metric, and D-JEPA learns relations among candidate outcomes~\cite{kang2026anisowm,liu2026djepa}. Data coverage matters as well: stable pushing depends on contact configurations, and active poking yields informative transitions~\cite{lynch1996pushing,agrawal2016poking,yu2016pushing}. DAgger aggregates expert labels at learner-visited states~\cite{ross2011dagger}, whereas DART injects and optimizes action noise during demonstration collection to expose recovery states~\cite{laskey2017dart}. Inspired by the latter and following AdaJEPA's contact-biased collection~\cite{wang2026adajepa}, we perturb interaction-directed actions with Gaussian noise of fixed scale, without expert relabeling or noise-distribution optimization. These approaches act once, during training, whereas ECP supplies a cost specific to the episode at hand.

\paragraph{Search and adaptation at test time.}
MPC repeatedly re-optimizes actions as observations arrive~\cite{garcia1989mpc}, and the cross-entropy method, its sample-efficient variants, and path-integral control strengthen this optimization~\cite{rubinstein1999cem,pinneri2021icem,williams2017mppi}. Test-time training and entropy minimization instead update the predictor under distribution shift~\cite{sun2020ttt,wang2021tent}; in control, AdaJEPA adapts world-model parameters from executed transitions, and OpTTA uses imagined goal-reaching cost during adaptation~\cite{wang2026adajepa,mete2026optta}. These methods improve the optimizer or the model; ECP instead changes the cost being optimized, and intervenes while model updates continue.

\paragraph{Memory and temporal structure in control.}
Steering an agent away from visited states is a classical idea: potential fields shape local motion but are prone to traps and oscillation~\cite{khatib1986potential,koren1991potential}, tabu search uses memory to discourage revisits during optimization~\cite{glover1989tabu}, and search on the replay buffer turns stored observations into navigational waypoints~\cite{eysenbach2019sorb}. Behavior and skill priors bias control with previous experience~\cite{tirumala2022behaviorpriors,pertsch2021spirl}, and ACT learns temporally extended action chunks from demonstrations and ensembles their predictions over time~\cite{zhao2023act}. ECP differs in the source of its information: its memory is built online from the states at which the current execution stalled and acts as a soft latent cost that persists across replans, without demonstrations or a learned skill space.
