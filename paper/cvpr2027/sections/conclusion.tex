\section{Conclusion}
ECP turns realized stagnation into a local cost on candidate rollouts and a temporary change in goal weighting. These corrections improve completion with frozen and adaptive dynamics and complement contact-rich training data. Matched executions and ablations illustrate two ways to recover: leaving a stalled region and completing the remaining goal component.

\paragraph{Future work.}
A trigger combining motion over several windows with model disagreement could distinguish stalls from useful low-displacement maneuvers. Factorial data ablations could vary contact bias and action noise under fixed data and training budgets. Evaluation on a shared holdout across independent training seeds would support uncertainty intervals for paired rescues and regressions~\cite{henderson2018matters,agarwal2021statistical}.
