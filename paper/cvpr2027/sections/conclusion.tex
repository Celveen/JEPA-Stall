\section{Conclusion}
We identified \emph{stalling} as a pervasive closed-loop failure mode of latent world-model planners caused by optimizing a Markovian goal objective without memory of execution. \emph{Execution-Conditioned Planning} (ECP) turns the episode's execution trace into a self-supervised corrective signal: detecting stagnation from realized latent progress, repelling trajectories from visited traps via a provably goal-preserving stagnation memory, and completing unfinished subspaces via temporary goal reweighting. Requiring no learned parameters and compatible with both frozen and online-adapted models, ECP paired with interaction-biased training improves manipulation, shift robustness, navigation, and deformable control across ResNet, LeWM, and DINO-WM controllers---showing that execution history is a vital resource for planning with learned world models (\cref{sec:pending}).
