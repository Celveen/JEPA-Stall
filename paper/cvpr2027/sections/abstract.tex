\begin{abstract}
Latent world models enable image-goal control by predicting the outcomes of candidate actions. Yet repeated replanning can settle into local cycles: the controller continues to act while revisiting configurations that do not complete the task. We identify execution history as a missing source of feedback for the planning objective and introduce execution-conditioned planning (ECP). ECP records observations at which progress stalls and adds a local trajectory regularizer that discourages returning to those configurations. A temporary increase in the proprioceptive goal weight helps the controller complete the remaining agent-position component. These corrections operate through action optimization and support both frozen world models and continued online adaptation. We combine them with interaction-biased data augmentation to improve the experience available during offline learning. On PushObj, the combined approach raises success from 43.7\% to 72.3\% with a frozen model and from 69.4\% to 76.9\% with AdaJEPA, under the same 30-replan budget. Paired navigation evaluations and component ablations further demonstrate the value of stagnation memory and conditional activation. ECP connects learned predictive dynamics with evidence from execution, improving task completion through complementary changes to training data and planning.
\end{abstract}
