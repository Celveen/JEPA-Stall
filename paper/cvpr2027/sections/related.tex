\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}
\paragraph{Latent world models for control.}
Dyna interleaves learning, planning, and execution~\cite{sutton1991dyna}. Learned-dynamics MPC replans with neural predictors and accounts for model uncertainty by sampling~\cite{nagabandi2018neural,chua2018pets}. In visual control, Embed to Control learns locally linear latent dynamics, and visual foresight plans through predicted images~\cite{watter2015embed,finn2017foresight}. World Models and Dreamer learn behaviors from imagined experience, PlaNet optimizes latent action trajectories, and TD-MPC2 combines latent planning with learned rewards and values~\cite{ha2018worldmodels,hafner2019planet,hafner2020dreamer,hansen2024tdmpc2}.

Joint-embedding prediction learns in representation space. I-JEPA predicts image features, V-JEPA extends feature prediction to video, and LeJEPA adds an isotropic Gaussian embedding regularizer~\cite{assran2023ijepa,bardes2024vjepa,balestriero2025lejepa}. DINO-WM plans in pretrained visual features, V-JEPA~2 supports image-goal robot control, and LeWorldModel jointly learns an encoder and predictor~\cite{zhou2025dinowm,assran2025vjepa2,maes2026lewm}. ECP is orthogonal to these advances: it uses their representations to decide, from execution, when and how the planning objective should change.

\paragraph{Representation geometry for planning.}
Representations can preserve rewards and transitions, fit a value-aware model objective, or support differentiable goal-directed planning~\cite{gelada2019deepmdp,farahmand2017valueaware,srinivas2018upn}. Inspired by perceptual straightening~\cite{henaff2019straightening}, Temporal Straightening aligns consecutive latent displacements to improve Euclidean planning geometry~\cite{wang2026straightening}. AnisoWM shapes the latent metric, and D-JEPA learns relations among candidate outcomes~\cite{kang2026anisowm,liu2026djepa}. These methods shape the latent space once, during training. ECP adapts the cost per episode: it measures realized movement, adds an execution-specific cost around stalled states, and reweights the visual and proprioceptive goal components during recovery.

\paragraph{Data coverage and interaction-rich learning.}
DART injects and optimizes action noise during demonstration collection to expose recovery states~\cite{laskey2017dart}. DAgger instead aggregates expert labels at learner-visited states~\cite{ross2011dagger}. Our training-side inspiration is DART's collection-time perturbation: we follow AdaJEPA's contact-biased recipe~\cite{wang2026adajepa} with fixed Gaussian noise, without expert relabeling or noise-distribution optimization. Stable pushing depends on contact configurations, and active poking supplies informative transitions~\cite{lynch1996pushing,agrawal2016poking,yu2016pushing}. The resulting observations and executed actions train a dynamics model.

\paragraph{Action chunks and temporal organization.}
ACT learns action sequences from demonstrations and temporally ensembles predictions for the same execution step~\cite{zhao2023act}. This inspires the temporal design of ECP, which optimizes actions through a world model and carries stagnation memory and temporary goal weighting across replans; our LeWM extension applies guidance in four-replan bursts (\cref{sec:backbone}).

\paragraph{Online adaptation and planning objectives.}
MPC repeatedly optimizes actions using new observations~\cite{garcia1989mpc}. The cross-entropy method, its sample-efficient variants, and path-integral control improve the action search~\cite{rubinstein1999cem,pinneri2021icem,williams2017mppi}. ECP instead changes the cost that the optimizer evaluates. Test-time training and entropy minimization update predictors under distribution shift~\cite{sun2020ttt,wang2021tent}. In control, AdaJEPA updates world-model parameters from executed transitions, and OpTTA uses imagined goal-reaching cost during adaptation~\cite{wang2026adajepa,mete2026optta}. ECP complements these updates and intervenes while they continue.

Behavior and skill priors guide movement using previous experience~\cite{tirumala2022behaviorpriors,pertsch2021spirl}. Classical potential fields shape local motion but can produce traps or oscillation~\cite{khatib1986potential,koren1991potential}. Tabu search uses memory to discourage revisits during optimization, and replay-buffer search uses stored observations as navigational waypoints~\cite{glover1989tabu,eysenbach2019sorb}. ECP adjusts its planning objective online with a soft latent cost around the states where the current physical execution has stalled, and requires neither demonstrations nor a learned skill space.
