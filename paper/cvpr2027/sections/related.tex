\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}
\paragraph{World models and latent planning.}
Planning through a learned model dates back to Dyna and neural MPC~\cite{sutton1991dyna,nagabandi2018neural,chua2018pets}. In visual control, it has progressed from locally linear embeddings and pixel prediction~\cite{watter2015embed,finn2017foresight} to the learned latent state spaces of World Models, PlaNet, Dreamer, and TD-MPC2~\cite{ha2018worldmodels,hafner2019planet,hafner2020dreamer,hansen2024tdmpc2}. Joint-embedding predictive architectures (JEPAs) predict representations directly~\cite{assran2023ijepa,bardes2024vjepa,balestriero2025lejepa}, and DINO-WM, V-JEPA~2, and LeWorldModel show that such predictors support zero-shot planning from goal images~\cite{zhou2025dinowm,assran2025vjepa2,maes2026lewm}. ECP is orthogonal: taking the world model as given, it decides from realized execution when and how the planning objective should change.

\paragraph{Shaping representations and offline data.}
A second line of work improves the metric space or training data before deployment. Representations can preserve rewards, transitions, or gradient planning paths~\cite{gelada2019deepmdp,farahmand2017valueaware,srinivas2018upn}. Building on perceptual straightening~\cite{henaff2019straightening}, Temporal Straightening aligns consecutive latent displacements~\cite{wang2026straightening}, while AnisoWM shapes directional metrics and D-JEPA learns decision-aligned relations~\cite{kang2026anisowm,liu2026djepa}. Data coverage is equally critical: stable pushing depends on contact configurations, and active poking yields informative transitions~\cite{lynch1996pushing,agrawal2016poking,yu2016pushing}. DAgger queries expert labels at learner-visited states~\cite{ross2011dagger}, whereas DART injects action noise during collection to expose recovery states~\cite{laskey2017dart}. Inspired by DART and following AdaJEPA's contact-biased collection~\cite{wang2026adajepa}, we perturb object-directed actions with fixed-scale Gaussian noise offline without expert relabeling. These approaches improve training coverage or representation geometry; ECP supplies an online cost tailored to the current episode.

\paragraph{Search and adaptation at test time.}
MPC repeatedly re-optimizes actions as observations arrive~\cite{garcia1989mpc}, aided by CEM, iCEM, and MPPI samplers~\cite{rubinstein1999cem,pinneri2021icem,williams2017mppi}. Under distribution shift, test-time training and entropy minimization update model parameters online~\cite{sun2020ttt,wang2021tent}; in control, AdaJEPA adapts world-model weights from executed transitions, and OpTTA uses optimistic goal-reaching cost during adaptation~\cite{wang2026adajepa,mete2026optta}. These methods improve search or adapt model parameters; ECP instead reshapes the objective itself and coordinates intervention with ongoing model updates via a gate that tracks prediction-loss improvement.

\paragraph{Memory and temporal structure in control.}
Steering an agent away from visited traps is a classic idea: potential fields shape local motion but suffer from local minima and goal-blocking when obstacles lie near the target~\cite{khatib1986potential,koren1991potential}, Tabu Search uses memory to discourage revisits~\cite{glover1989tabu}, and SoRB turns stored observations into waypoints~\cite{eysenbach2019sorb}. Behavior and skill priors bias control with prior experience~\cite{tirumala2022behaviorpriors,pertsch2021spirl}, and ACT executes temporally extended action chunks to reduce compounding errors~\cite{zhao2023act}. ECP differs in both source and structure: its memory is built online solely from states where the current execution stalled, scales its radius with goal distance to exclude the goal outside the numerical floor, and persists across replans without demonstrations or learned priors.
